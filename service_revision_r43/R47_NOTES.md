# Full-method neighbor audit and origin conventions (11 October 2026)

Ong et al., Thermal Science and Engineering Progress 77 (2026) 104852,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsep.2026.104852, and Wang et al., Applied Energy 410
(2026) 127558, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2026.127558, were read through
their complete methods/results/appendices on 11 October 2026. Both already
model physical heat services and joint process behavior. They are more complete
than this certificate model for joint plant electricity/dispatch. Pinch,
fixed input, common heat, heat pumps and hourly dispatch are not our novelty.
The bounded contribution is the declared-family two-sided adaptive heat-service
certificate and its independently evaluated unresolved capacity gap.

datasets/near_neighbor_inputs_r47_20261011.json contains only a few attributed
printed numerical facts with page/table locators. The new script does not
access or reproduce their private plant series, original process software,
surrogate weights, absolute plant profits or legal allowance settlements.
Printed Ong source variants are not fitted together. Wang's pooled subtraction
is compared with a homogeneous physical origin balance, not called proof that
the whole paper is invalid. Three two-period covariance counterexamples are
explicitly synthetic scenarios; the 56 origin grid rows are deterministic
identity tests, not observations or confidence intervals. The fossil-only and
intentionally pooled rewards use the same central heat path and cost
assumptions. Assigning a fossil-only value to all mixed carbon is not an
alternative valid fossil-only convention. Source PDFs are not redistributed.

The mixed-stream eligibility discussion uses the official 1 January 2025
EU MRR consolidation, Article 49(1) and (6), checked 11 October 2026:
https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2018/2066/2025-01-01/eng
Its proportional non-zero-rated share is not automatically all mixed captured
carbon; zero-rating criteria and actual chain eligibility require separate
evidence. Our life-cycle retention scenario is not a legal settlement algorithm.
